# Business Problem Statement

## Background

The business operates 129 active vendors and 268K distinct products across a multi-store liquor retail network. Purchasing, sales, and inventory are each recorded in separate transactional systems — purchase orders, point-of-sale, and stock records — with no single view connecting vendor cost, selling price, and stock position. Leadership can see that revenue is growing, but has no consolidated, statistically validated answer for which vendors are actually driving profit, whether current purchasing habits are cost-efficient, or how much capital is sitting in inventory that isn't moving.

## Business Objective

Answer, in one place: which vendors drive the majority of revenue and profit, which vendors are underperforming, whether purchase volume and vendor selection meaningfully affect unit cost, whether inventory levels are aligned with actual demand, and which products are tying up disproportionate working capital.

## Workstreams

### Problem 1 — Vendor Performance & Profitability Analysis
Identify which vendors drive the majority of revenue and profit, and quantify the margin gap between high- and low-performing vendors.

### Problem 2 — Procurement Cost Optimization
Determine whether purchase volume and vendor selection have a statistically significant effect on unit procurement cost.

### Problem 3 — Inventory Health & Working Capital Analysis
Assess whether inventory levels are aligned with sales demand, and identify which products are tying up disproportionate inventory value.

### Problem 4 — Statistical Validation & Business Benchmarking
Validate every pattern with correlation analysis, distribution/outlier detection, hypothesis testing, and confidence intervals — so recommendations are backed by evidence, not assumption.

### Problem 5 — Executive Performance Monitoring
Consolidate vendor, procurement, and inventory findings into a single executive view of overall business health.

## Final Deliverable

A 3-page Power BI dashboard (`04_PowerBI/`): Executive Overview, Vendor & Procurement Analytics, Inventory & Product Analytics — backed by a 48-question SQL analysis, a full Python statistical validation layer, and a business conclusion report with data-driven recommendations.
